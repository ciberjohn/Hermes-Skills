## Portuguese-Language Gap

**Key finding:** No major European Portuguese cybersecurity or AI trainer exists at scale. Brazilian Portuguese trainers exist (e.g. Cod3r on Udemy) but:
- They use Brazilian spelling and vocabulary
- They don't cover UK/EU cybersecurity regulations (GDPR, NCSC, ICO)
- They don't target the European market

**This is a genuine blue ocean opportunity.**

## Course Structure Best Practices

| Element | Standard | Source |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Video length | 5–10 min (never over 12) | Multiple platforms, instructor interviews |
| Module size | 6–8 videos per module | W3C, Coursera |
| Lab time | 2:1 to 3:1 ratio to video time | Cisco Netacad |
| Course length | 6–12 modules, 30–60 min video each | Industry standard |
| Quiz frequency | 1 per module (3–5 questions) | Pedagogical research |

## Lab Design (Cisco Netacad Model)

| Phase | Time | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Guided | 40% | Walk-through with exact steps |
| Scaffolded | 35% | Adapt pattern, hints available |
| Challenge | 25% | Independent problem, requirements only |

## UK-Specific Sales Channels

- **Apprenticeship Levy** — £7k–27k per learner, government-funded. Requires approved training provider status.
- **CCS Framework** (Crown Commercial Service) — Government procurement. High barrier but high reward.
- **NCSC Certified Training** — Cyber security certification pathway. Gold standard in UK government.
- **LinkedIn Outreach** — Direct to IT/Training managers. Highest ROI for initial B2B sales.
