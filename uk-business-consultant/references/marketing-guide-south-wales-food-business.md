## 2. Marketing Portuguese Cuisine to a Welsh Valleys Audience

### The Positioning Strategy
**Don't sell "Portuguese food" — sell "the best thing you'll taste this week"**

- **Bridge the cultural gap**: Welsh valleys have strong food traditions (Welsh cakes, bara brith, cawl). Compare Portuguese foods to familiar Welsh ones:
  - Pastel de nata = "like a Welsh custard tart but elevated"
  - Bolinhos de bacalhau = "salt cod fritters — think Welsh rarebit meets calamari"
  - Bifanas = "Portuguese pork bap — our answer to a Welsh breakfast bap"
  - Pão de Deus = "Portuguese brioche with coconut — like a Welsh tea cake"

- **The cultural story angle**: Frame Sara's Portuguese heritage as a unique asset — "authentic Portuguese recipes passed down through generations, now in Mountain Ash"
- **Portuguese community in Wales**: Welsh valleys have a small but notable Portuguese-speaking community (especially around Merthyr/Cardiff). Target them as initial customer base — they'll spread the word
- **Taste test events**: Free samples at local markets — let the product speak. Portuguese baking is objectively delicious and sells itself on taste

### Key Messaging Hooks
- "Portuguese sunshine in the Welsh valleys"
- "Authentic pastéis de nata — made in Mountain Ash"
- "From Lisbon to the Cynon Valley"
- "Europe's best-kept baking secret, now local"

### Cultural Event Tie-Ins
- **Portugal Day** (June 10): Special offers, social media campaign
- **Welsh St David's Day** (March 1): Portuguese-Welsh fusion specials
- **Eurovision / Football tournaments**: Portuguese-themed party boxes
- **Christmas**: Portuguese bolo rei (king cake) + Welsh mince pies combo

## 7. Pricing Psychology for Premium Homemade Food

### Pricing Principles for Portuguese Baked Goods
| Item | Suggested Price | Cost Estimate | Margin |
|------|----------------|---------------|--------|
| Pastel de nata (single) | £3.00-£3.50 | ~£0.50 | 83-86% |
| Pastel de nata (box of 6) | £15-£18 | ~£3.00 | 80-83% |
| Bolinhos de bacalhau (6) | £12-£15 | ~£3.50 | 71-77% |
| Bolo de bolacha (cake) | £25-£35 | ~£6.00 | 76-83% |
| Catering platter (serves 10-12) | £45-£60 | ~£12 | 73-80% |
| Wedding dessert table (50 portions) | £250-£400 | ~£60 | 76-85% |

### Psychological Pricing Tactics
- **Anchoring**: Show the box of 6 at £18, then single at £3.50 — the single seems reasonable by comparison
- **Charm pricing**: £3.00 feels cheaper than £3.50, but for premium, round numbers convey quality: £3.50, £18, £35
- **The "free" add-on**: "Every catering order includes a free Portuguese custard tart tasting" — adds perceived value at near-zero cost
- **Tiered pricing**: Small box £12, Medium £22, Large £35 — people default to middle option
- **Limited availability**: "Only baking 30 dozen this week" creates scarcity
- **Bundle pricing**: "Coffee + Pastel de nata = £4" for market stalls — bundles increase basket size

### Why You Can Charge Premium
- Authentic Portuguese baking is **unique in RCT** — no direct competitor
- Homemade, small-batch = higher perceived quality than mass-produced
- Imported ingredients (Portuguese custard powder, port wine for sauces) justify premium
- The story (Portuguese heritage, traditional recipe) adds value

## 8. Building a Waitlist/Pre-Order Model (£0)

### The Pre-Order System (Free via Google Forms or Instagram)
1. Create a Google Form (free): Customer name, email, what they want, quantity, collection/delivery date
2. Link in Instagram bio: "Order your pasteis de nata — pre-order for Saturday collection"
3. **Monday**: Post "Taking orders for this week's bakes. Link in bio to order. Cut-off Thursday 6pm."
4. **Friday-Saturday**: Bake to order, no waste, no unsold stock
5. **Sunday-Monday**: "Sold out this week! New orders open Monday" — scarcity builds demand

### WhatsApp Broadcast List (Free)
- Add customers to a WhatsApp broadcast list (not group — they can't see each other)
- Send weekly: "This week's menu — fresh Portuguese pastries. Order by Thursday."
- WhatsApp is the most-used app in the UK — 98% open rate
- Use WhatsApp Business app (free) for automatic replies and quick replies

### Waitlist Tiers
- **Priority List**: First access to weekly orders (text them before posting publicly)
- **VIP List**: Customers who've ordered 3+ times — get first dibs on holiday/event orders
- **Referral Rewards**: "Refer a friend who orders, get 10% off your next order"

### Order Confirmation via WhatsApp (Free)
- Template: "Hi [name], your order of 12 pasteis de nata is confirmed for Saturday collection at 11am. Total: £36. Cash or bank transfer on collection. Address: [street]. See you Saturday!"
