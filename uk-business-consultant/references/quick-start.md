# Quick Start — Using the UK Business Consultant Skill

## Step 1: Load the skill
```
skill_view(name='uk-business-consultant')
```

## Step 2: Ask for business advice
Simply message your Hermes agent with your business question:

- "I'm thinking of selling pastéis de nata from home. Is this viable?"
- "What businesses could I start with £700?"
- "Walk me through what we need legally to sell homemade cakes."
- "Give me a financial model for a weekend baking business."
- "Generate 5 business ideas combining admin skills and Portuguese baking."

## Step 3: Get a structured report
The skill can email a full professional advisory report to any address.
Just ask: "Email me the full assessment of this idea."

## What This Skill Won't Do
- File taxes or register businesses for you
- Provide legal advice (will recommend a solicitor)
- Recommend loans or debt
- Give generic advice without understanding your specific context
