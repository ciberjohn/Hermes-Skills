# Banned Words, Phrases, and Openers

Statistically flagged as AI-generated text markers across multiple studies (Cheng et al. 2025, Russell, Karpinska & Iyyer 2025, Juzek & Ward 2025, Wikipedia's Signs of AI Writing field guide, and community detection patterns on X and Reddit). Never use any of these. Replace with concrete alternatives or restructure the sentence.

## Structural Tells (not greppable — manual review)

- Rule of three: exactly three items/paragraphs grouped in a row
- Three consecutive sentences of the same length
- Parataxis: three or more short declarative sentences in a row
- Hedging seesaw: every claim balanced with a counterclaim of equal weight
- Paired oppositions: "measured in X, not Y" / "it is not X, it is Y"
- Uniform paragraph structure: topic sentence, explanation, example, transition
- Passive voice: "is being done", "was found to be", "are considered to be"
- Corporate pep talk tone: cheerleading with no specifics
- Every paragraph ends with a transition or summary
